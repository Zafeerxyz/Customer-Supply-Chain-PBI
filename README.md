# 📦 Customer Supply Chain Analytics — Power BI

An end-to-end **Customer & Supply Chain Analytics** project built with **Microsoft Power BI**, focused on understanding customer behavior, sales performance, shipping operations, and supply-chain efficiency through interactive business intelligence dashboards.

The project transforms supply-chain data into actionable insights that can help business teams monitor performance, identify operational issues, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

Supply-chain businesses generate large amounts of customer, order, product, and logistics data. However, raw data alone does not provide clear answers to important business questions.

This project uses Power BI to create an interactive analytical solution that helps answer questions such as:

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
* Which regions contribute the most to business performance?
* How efficiently are orders being delivered?
* Where are delivery delays occurring?
* Which shipping methods perform better?
* What areas of the supply chain require attention?

The dashboard provides stakeholders with a centralized view of **customer, sales, and supply-chain performance**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify high-value customers
* Analyze product and category performance
* Evaluate shipping and delivery performance
* Identify delivery risks and operational inefficiencies

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard is designed around important business KPIs such as:

| KPI                       | Purpose                             |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Total Sales**           | Measures overall revenue generation |
| **Total Orders**          | Measures order volume               |
| **Total Customers**       | Measures customer base              |
| **Average Order Value**   | Measures average revenue per order  |
| **Profit**                | Measures financial performance      |
| **Profit Margin**         | Measures profitability              |
| **On-Time Delivery**      | Measures delivery reliability       |
| **Late Delivery Risk**    | Identifies operational risk         |
| **Customer Contribution** | Identifies high-value customers     |
